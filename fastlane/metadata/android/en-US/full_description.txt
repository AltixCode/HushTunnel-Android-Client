HushTunnel is a privacy-focused VPN service. VPN connectivity is the app's core and primary functionality.

HushTunnel uses Android's VpnService to create a secure device-level tunnel from your Android device to the HushTunnel server you select. Traffic between the device and tunnel endpoint is encrypted. Traffic after the endpoint retains the protection provided by the destination protocol, such as HTTPS.

Privacy commitments:
• No browsing-history logs
• No DNS-query logs
• No traffic-destination logs
• No VPN payload or message-content logs
• No sale, use, or disclosure of VPN traffic data to third parties beyond transient processing required to provide the requested tunnel
• No advertising SDKs and no traffic manipulation for advertising or monetization
• Only aggregate byte totals are retained for plan quota enforcement

Before first use, HushTunnel displays a separate VPN disclosure explaining how VpnService processes traffic. You may decline; the VPN will not start. You can disconnect or remove VPN permission at any time.

Account information, subscription state, aggregate quota use, and necessary purchase records are processed only to provide and secure the service. In-app purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. HushTunnel never receives full payment-card details.

Features include encrypted tunnel connectivity, server selection, native subscriptions, wallet funding, purchase restoration, account controls, and permanent in-app account deletion.
